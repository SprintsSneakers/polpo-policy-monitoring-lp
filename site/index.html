Frequently asked questions

What will I receive?
A Polpo trial account with saved searches and email alerts configured around your topic. We review the setup with you and hand over access during the 30-minute onboarding session.
What happens after I complete the form?
We use your answers to prepare your account and arrange the onboarding session with you. Submitting the form requests your trial; monitoring starts after the onboarding session and account handover.
Why is the onboarding session included?
The session is held in your own prepared account. We check your topic and alert settings, show you how to use and adjust the monitor, and give you access to continue independently.
When will I receive updates?
Monitoring starts immediately after handover. An email alert is sent when matching information is available, according to your alert settings. The first new alert therefore depends on relevant publications and your chosen frequency.
Can I change my topic or alerts?
Yes. You can adjust your saved searches and alert preferences yourself. We show you how during the onboarding session.
Which political sources are included?
We agree the relevant institutions and available sources for your topic when preparing the account. You can see the selected sources in your trial.
What happens to my details?
Your name, work email and topic go to Polpo so we can prepare your account and plan the onboarding session. We do not sell or pass them on. Every email carries an unsubscribe link and we delete your details whenever you ask.
